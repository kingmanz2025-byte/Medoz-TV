# Medoz TV — Design Proposal

## Design direction
A premium Arabic-first Samsung TV companion experience using deep navy, restrained gold accents, cinematic imagery, generous spacing, and readable RTL typography. The visual direction is original; it must not reuse Morph TV branding, code, or protected assets.

## Dashboard
- Fixed RTL sidebar with a clear active section.
- Large cinematic hero panel with title, short description, and one primary action.
- Large service cards with artwork and clear focus states.
- Compact clock/date, prayer schedule, next-prayer countdown, and weather card.
- Favorites and recently used items where relevant.

## Core visual principles
- Navy is the base; gold is reserved for focus, selection, and important indicators.
- Use a dark overlay behind text on imagery.
- Keep cards aligned, spacious, and legible from a couch.
- All navigation must work with a TV remote; no mouse-only interactions.
- Avoid excessive motion, layered blur, and heavy assets that may slow the TV.

## Approval status
This is a design direction and requirements document, not proof of a working implementation. Compare the implemented screen with the approved reference before coding additional screens.
