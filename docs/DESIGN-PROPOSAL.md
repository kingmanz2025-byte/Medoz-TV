# Medoz TV — Design Proposal

## Approved Design Decisions
- Colors: Deep navy and gold.
- Home screen: Clock, date, prayer times, and large service cards.
- Offline-first: Yes, wherever technically possible.
- Language: Arabic with right-to-left layout.
- Navigation: Large, readable cards suitable for TV remote control.
- Identity: Original Medoz TV design, independent from Morph TV branding.
- Cost: Free, with no subscriptions or paid themes.

## Proposed Home Screen
1. Clock, date, and connection status.
2. Prayer times and optional adhan.
3. Large cards for Quran, azkar, ruqyah, radio, and favorites.
4. Settings for themes, audio sources, family profiles, and privacy.
5. Weather when an internet connection is available.

## Offline Support
- Save preferences, themes, and favorites locally.
- Support local audio playback where compatible.
- Keep clock and date available using device time.
- Retain saved prayer-time settings.
- Clearly indicate online-only features.

## Android Companion
- TV discovery and connection status.
- Remote-control functions where supported.
- Installation assistance using compatible methods.

## Compatibility
Test the exact Samsung TV model and Tizen version before promising installation or feature support.

## Approval Status
The navy-and-gold palette, home screen concept, and offline-first priority are approved.

Final screen mockups and the complete feature scope still require approval. **Do not begin programming until the final design is approved.**
